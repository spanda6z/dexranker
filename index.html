What this actually is

DexRanker is a distribution service. We place your token in front of people who already browse Dexscreener and Solana trading communities. We do not create volume, we do not control the Dexscreener algorithm, and we do not promise rankings.

Rankings move based on real trading activity, unique wallets, liquidity, and market conditions. Our job is to increase the chance that the right eyes see your token while those conditions are forming.

FAQ

Straight answers

Do you guarantee a ranking?
No. Dexscreener rankings are driven by real trading activity, unique wallets, liquidity, and their internal scoring. We increase exposure. The market and the algorithm determine rank.
Is this volume or wash trading?
No. We do not create artificial volume or engage in wash trading. This is a distribution and visibility service only.
How fast does a campaign start?
Most campaigns begin within a few hours of confirmed payment and token details. Timing can vary slightly based on channel availability.
What do I need to provide?
Token contract address, preferred package, and a contact method (Telegram recommended). Optionally any notes about timing.
When is the best time to run a campaign?
When your pair already has some organic volume and liquidity. Distribution amplifies existing interest. Running cold with near-zero activity is rarely effective.
